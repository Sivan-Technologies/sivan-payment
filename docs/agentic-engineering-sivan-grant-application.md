## Step 2: Details

Project Details:
Sivan Technologies is building Sivan Payment AI: an autonomous, multi-channel payment AI agent operating across Telegram and WhatsApp. In global trade, freelancing, and digital services, informal messaging conversations often lead to unverified payment claims, dispute friction, and complex manual checkout forms.

Sivan Payment AI eliminates these frictions by allowing users to manage payments directly inside their chat apps using natural language. Users can type commands like "Draft a service agreement with +2348012345678 for logo design, $15, deliver in 2 days" or "send 10 usdc to W7ydftpwxsEE7732N2w5UTHDFrBvYUDmAeuGCZwPDu7".

Sivan Payment AI handles 100% of the cognitive parsing, identity resolution, contract state machine execution, double-spend protection, and on-chain settlement verification on Solana, providing users with a 1-tap balance payment option and instant Solscan proof links.

This is a non-custodial, compliance-first architecture. Settlement and banking infrastructure are handled through regulated payment gateways and local payout partners. Sivan Payment AI provides autonomous transaction coordination, natural language intent processing, agreement tracking, real-time push alerts, and verified audit logs.
