## Field 1: HOW DO YOU KNOW PEOPLE ACTUALLY NEED, OR WILL NEED THIS PRODUCT?

We know this need from direct market realities in emerging markets and the rapid rise of AI agents:

1. Emerging Market Freelance Economy: Over 80% of digital service commerce in Nigeria and across Africa happens informally within messaging apps like Telegram and WhatsApp. This informal setup creates constant friction: clients fear paying upfront before work is verified, and service providers fear non-payment after delivery. Fake bank receipts and payment delays are widespread, while cross-border wire transfers charge high fees and take days.

2. The Autonomous Agent Shift: AI agents are evolving from conversational chatbots into autonomous economic actors. As agents hire contractors, procure compute, and contract other agents, they need a native protocol to structure commitments, verify delivery, and settle funds trustlessly without manual web form friction.

Pilot testing confirmed that moving agreements into a chat-native, verifiable state machine eliminates dispute anxiety and gives both parties immediate confidence.

## Field 3: WHO ELSE IS BUILDING IN THIS SPACE, AND WHAT DO YOU THINK THEY'RE GETTING WRONG?

Current market players fall into three categories, all missing the core need:

1. Telegram Trading Bots (BonkBot, Trojan, Maestro): Highly optimized for meme coin sniping and token swaps, but completely lack infrastructure for multi-party commercial terms, milestone tracking, or delivery verification.
2. Web3 Freelance Portals (Gibwork, Superteam Earn): Desktop-bound web dApps requiring browser extensions and manual wallet connections. They ignore the reality that emerging market commerce lives inside mobile chat apps.
3. Merchant Payment Gateways (Sphere, Helio): Built for one-way e-commerce checkouts without bilateral Service Agreements, conditional holds, or dispute mediation.

What they get wrong: They force users out of their natural communication workflow into complex web interfaces and fail to connect crypto settlement with local fiat off-ramps. Sivan Payment AI brings trustless state machines directly into chat where deals actually happen.
