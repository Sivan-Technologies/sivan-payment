## About Sivan Payment AI (Comprehensive Grant & Pitch Dossier)

### One-Line Summary:
Sivan Payment AI is an agentic transaction coordination infrastructure enabling people, businesses, and AI agents to create, track, fund, and settle multi-channel Service Agreements on Solana.

### Core Problem Solved:
Over 80% of digital service commerce and freelance transactions in emerging markets take place informally within conversational apps like Telegram and WhatsApp. This informal environment creates severe counterparty risk, payment disputes, fake transfer claims, and delivery delays. At the same time, autonomous AI agents lack native infrastructure to negotiate terms, hold funds, and settle commitments safely.

### The Solution:
Sivan Payment AI models service arrangements as verifiable, deterministic state machines (Draft -> Pending -> Funded -> Delivered -> Released / Disputed). Users and AI agents interact naturally through conversational interfaces or APIs, while Sivan Payment AI handles:
1. Natural language intent parsing into strict transaction payloads in under 15ms.
2. Solana SPL token (USDC) funding and x402 payment facilities.
3. Automated milestone tracking with instant delivery push alerts.
4. One-tap balance transfers and instant settlement releases.
5. Unified multi-channel identity mapping and compliance tier controls.

### Technology Stack:
- Blockchain: Solana Devnet and Mainnet (SPL Token Program, USDC / USDT)
- Backend: Node.js, Fastify, TypeScript, PostgreSQL (Neon DB with composite indexing)
- Frontend and Console: Next.js, Vercel, Tailwind CSS, REST APIs
- Messaging Integrations: Telegram Bot API (@SivanAi_bot), WhatsApp Business API

### Traction and Proven Delivery:
- Live multi-channel state machine with 100% completed agreement settlement rate.
- Sub-50ms audit logging query speeds and 0.18ms ledger write latency.
- Verified on-chain Solana transactions for agreement creation, funding, and release.
- Live administrative management hub deployed on Vercel with real-time risk controls.
