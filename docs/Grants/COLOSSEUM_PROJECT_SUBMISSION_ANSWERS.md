## Field 2: WHAT ARE YOU BUILDING, AND WHO IS IT FOR?

Sivan Payment AI is an agentic transaction coordination infrastructure that enables individuals, businesses, and autonomous AI agents to create, track, fund, and settle verifiable Service Agreements on Solana.

Target Audience:
1. Emerging Market Creators and Freelancers: Millions of digital service transactions in Nigeria and across Africa happen informally across messaging apps (Telegram, WhatsApp) without structured agreements or payment protection.
2. SMEs and Agencies: Businesses needing structured milestone tracking, instant 1-tap balance funding, and automated settlement releases.
3. Autonomous AI Agents: AI agents executing commercial tasks need programmable identity, state machines, and trustless USDC settlement.

Sivan Payment AI models arrangements as deterministic state machines (Draft -> Pending -> Funded -> Delivered -> Released) accessible via natural language chat (@Sivan_Ai), WhatsApp, and REST APIs.

## Field 3: WHY DID YOU DECIDE TO BUILD THIS, AND WHY BUILD IT NOW?

We built Sivan Payment AI to solve two converging problems:

1. Emerging Market Payment Friction: In Africa, over 80% of informal digital commerce occurs in chat apps where trust deficits, payment delays, fake bank alerts, and missing records cause massive friction. Traditional banking wires take days and charge heavy fees. Sivan Payment AI turns casual conversations into legally clear, verifiable Service Agreements backed by instant USDC settlement on Solana.

2. The Rise of Autonomous Agentic Commerce: AI agents are shifting from text generation to commercial execution—hiring developers, procuring compute, and contracting with other agents. Existing payment gateways require manual web forms designed solely for humans. Agents need a native protocol to structure commitments and verify delivery.

Why now: Solana sub-second finality and negligible fees, combined with breakthroughs in conversational AI, make instant agentic settlement feasible at global scale today.
