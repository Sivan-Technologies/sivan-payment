## Field 5: YOU COULD BE WORKING ON ANYTHING RIGHT NOW — WHY CHOOSE THIS?

We chose this because we are at a rare inflection point where two massive technological shifts intersect: the rise of autonomous AI agents and the maturity of high-speed crypto rails (Solana).

In emerging markets like Nigeria and across Africa, over 80% of digital trade happens informally in WhatsApp and Telegram chats. Freelancers, agencies, and clients lose thousands of dollars every day to non-payment, fake receipts, and broken cross-border wires. 

Simultaneously, autonomous AI agents are moving from text generation to commercial execution. When an AI agent needs to hire a human developer, procure compute, or contract another agent, it cannot fill out a manual web form or wait 3 days for a wire transfer. 

Sivan Payment AI solves both problems with a single unified state-machine architecture. Building the commercial coordination layer that connects conversational emerging markets and autonomous AI agents directly to Solana is the highest-leverage problem we can dedicate our lives to solving.

## Field 6: TELL US ABOUT A TIME YOU WENT TO EXTREME LENGTHS TO ACCOMPLISH SOMETHING

During early architecture testing of Sivan Payment AI, external payment webhooks and unoptimized database queries were causing 6-second latency spikes under concurrent load, threatening our sub-second chat experience. 

Instead of accepting cloud infrastructure cost inflation or vendor workarounds, I spent 72 straight hours profiling database lock contention and rewriting the entire data access layer. I designed custom composite indexes, implemented synchronous in-memory rate limiting, and built a bounded audit ledger pipeline. 

This dropped our write latency down to 0.18ms, cut audit query speeds to under 50ms, and reduced webhook response times from 6,000ms to 18ms. I do not stop until technical systems achieve deterministic reliability, extreme speed, and battle-tested resilience.
