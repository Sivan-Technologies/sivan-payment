## 📌 SECTION 2: PROBLEM & AFRICAN BUSINESS IMPACT

### 2.1 What business problem in Abuja / Nigeria does Sivan payment Ai solve?
> Small and medium enterprises (SMEs) and freelancers in Abuja and across Nigeria conduct over 80% of their sales and service negotiations inside chat applications like WhatsApp and Telegram. However, these businesses face severe challenges:
> - Prepayment Risk: Buyers are reluctant to send bank transfers before receiving goods/services.
> - Default Risk: Service providers are frequently stiffed after delivering work.
> - Manual Reconciliation: Tracking invoices, bank alerts, and work milestones manually in chat creates extreme administrative overhead.
>
> Sivan payment Ai Solution:  
> Sivan payment Ai converts conversational agreements into automated, verifiable Service Agreements. Buyers deposit Naira or USDC into a locked virtual account. When the provider submits work proof, Sivan payment Ai dispatches real-time pop-up notification cards to both parties, automatically releasing Naira directly to the provider's Nigerian bank account upon approval.
